Meet The Artist

The Story Behind Maquillage by Abyna

Dedicated to unveiling your natural radiance, preserving bridal tranquility, and crafting timeless memories for your most cherished celebration.

Accra Based in Greater Accra & Available Worldwide
Founder & Lead Bridal Artist

“I believe every bride deserves to feel both breathtaking and utterly herself.”

Welcome! I’m Abyna, the creative force and lead artist behind Maquillage by Abyna. Over the years, I have had the profound honor of glamming more than a hundred brides as they celebrate two of life’s most milestone occasions: their vibrant Traditional Ghanaian engagements and their White wedding ceremonies.

My signature aesthetic was born out of a desire for authentic elegance. I observed too many brides feeling masked by heavy, uncomfortable foundations that cracked in tropical heat or looked stark under photography lighting. My mission was clear: to pioneer a skin-first, breathable, luxury makeup method that accentuates bone structure, respects melanin undertones, and feels as weightless as silk.

Whether we are harmonizing your look to the intricate royal weaves of Kente, balancing henna with gold accessories for an Islamic ceremony, or creating a dewy, soft-focus aisle glam, every stroke is executed with intention, hygiene, and perfectionism.

Artistry Principles

The Three Pillars of Our Craft

What sets the Maquillage by Abyna experience apart from everyday salon makeup.

1. Skin-First Longevity

Ghana's climate requires specialized skin preparation. We utilize lightweight, multi-tiered hydration primers, barrier-locking mists, and oil-balancing formulations that ensure 16 hours of crease-free wear through high humidity, emotional tears, and dancing.

2. The Calm Morning Experience

Your wedding morning sets the emotional tone for your entire celebration. We arrive punctually, curate a peaceful environment with soft music and positive energy, and coordinate seamlessly with your photographer and hairstylist to eliminate rush and stress.

3. Melanin Color Harmony

No two complexions are identical. We possess exhaustive mastery of warm, golden, neutral, and deep olive African skin undertones. We ensure seamless face-to-neck continuity with absolutely zero camera flashback or ashy undertones.

What To Expect

Your Wedding Morning Experience

A meticulously choreographed glam timeline designed so you never feel rushed.

01

Arrival & Calming Atmosphere

We arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled glam call-time to setup clean, sanitized workstations, ring lights, and comfortable staging in your bridal suite.

02

Skin Prep & Collagen Mask Ritual

Lymphatic depuffing facial massage, customized hydrogel eye treatments, and intense hydration infusions to give your skin an illuminated, glass-like base.

03

Bespoke Artistry & Lash Application

Carefully sculpting the eyes, customizing faux mink lashes to your eye curvature, perfecting the complexion, and matching your lip tone to your bridal florals and dress.

04

Dressing, Veil Support & Touch-Up Kit

Body radiance application for your collarbones and shoulders, assisting with your gown or Kente wrapping, and handing over your personalized morning touch-up kit.
